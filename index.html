Deyang Chen

PhD Candidate in Marine and Atmospheric Sciences at the State University of New York at Stony Brook (SoMAS), advised by Minghao Qiu. My research sits at the intersection of climate change, air quality, and public health, using frontier statistical models and air quality models to understand how climate change affects human health through air quality. I am also interested in other impacts of climate change.
